At MCN 2026, I joined a panel of speakers to discuss a pivotal question: how do we thoughtfully evolve our online collection interfaces — and where do we invest first? Do we prioritize AI-powered search? AI-augmented records? Or user-facing conversational features that invite visitors to explore collections on their own terms? Each path carries different implications for trust, accuracy, and long-term sustainability. With my group of panelists we shared real-world collection redesign projects, our frameworks for evaluating AI features and functionality, and our roadmaps for future integration. We also explored what becomes possible when we think beyond institutional boundaries — sharing technical approaches, case studies, and object data across institutions.
